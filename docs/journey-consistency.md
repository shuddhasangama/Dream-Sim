# Journey consistency update

Scope: verification copy and agreement scope, health filters, date readiness and
Guru modes. No payment/booking lifecycle, signup or engagement/marriage changes.

Health: Show all pauses health filtering, restore reinstates saved preferences.
No filter includes unknown disclosures; selected categories keep the explicit
unknown toggle. Broad sensory includes hearing/vision; broad non-visible includes
neurodivergence/mental health; broad cancer includes current/past cancer. Narrow
preferences never infer what an unspecified broad disclosure means. Trauma is
not treated as equivalent to every mental-health condition. No preference is
inferred from the user's own health. Matching remains mutual.

Date readiness: shared projection distinguishes proposed, awaiting partner,
confirmed, completed and cancellation/no-show. Proposal access is kept so users
can sign; the navigation milestone says Date plan available, not date confirmed.
No clock alone marks a date completed. Home, Week, date plan and Guru consume
saved state. Restaurant confirmation is a separate pending integration.

Identity: capture, provider identity result and BGV are separate. Every agreement
still records its own identity step and signature. Existing valid identity results
can be reused with an explicit agreement action. This change does not introduce
new mandatory camera captures or claim live liveness checks. Test approval remains
labelled as testing and never becomes BGV verification.

Guru: guided actions labelled independently of optional AI conversation. Each
reply retains its actual mode; status answers are guided even with AI enabled.
Date preparation prompts are not offered as an agreement-stage topic.

Deploy Railway and rebuild Codemagic for mobile UI. No new database migration.
