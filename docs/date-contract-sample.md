This agreement is entered into by **[User A]** and **[User B]** (each a "Participant") through the DREAM platform ("the Platform"), governing a single scheduled date instance. It nests under the broader Dating-stage "getting-to-know" agreement and does not itself constitute a relationship, exclusivity, or any longer-term commitment between Participants.

### 3. Payment & Fees
- Payment opens once both calendar availability and venue/slot are confirmed — not before.
- Per-date fee of **[₹ amount]**, or coverage under an active date-subscription plan.
- Fees are processed through the Platform's payment system; Participants do not exchange payment details directly for the purposes of this booking.

### 4. Bill Split (as selected at booking)
One of: **50/50 · alternate-treats · host-pays · pay-your-own.** The selected method is binding for this date instance unless both Participants agree to change it before the date occurs.

### 5. Cancellation & No-Show Policy
*(Modeled on standard service-platform cancellation frameworks — e.g. rideshare/booking cancellation-fee structures.)*
- Cancellations made **more than [X hours]** before the scheduled time: no fee.
- Cancellations inside the notice window, or no-shows: a cancellation fee of **[₹ amount / % of booking]** may apply, payable to the other Participant and/or withheld from the cancelling Participant's payment method.
- Repeated late cancellations or no-shows count toward the Compliance Rating (Section 7) and may restrict future booking privileges.

### 6. Code of Conduct
Participants agree to:
- Treat each other with respect; harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior of any kind is prohibited.
- Not solicit the other Participant's contact information, employment, financial participation, or any activity outside the Platform's stated purpose during the date.
- Not record, photograph, or share content of the date without the other Participant's consent.

### 7. Background Verification & Compliance Rating
*(Modeled on service-platform trust & safety norms — e.g. rideshare driver/rider rating systems.)*
- This match and date were facilitated in reliance on the Background Verification (BGV) check completed at sign-up. Participants confirm the accuracy of their submitted information remains current.
- Following each date, Participants may rate one another on conduct and adherence to this plan, similar to rating systems used on service-oriented platforms.
- A pattern of low ratings, verified misconduct reports, or Playbook violations may result in warnings, temporary suspension, or permanent removal from the Platform, independent of any single match's outcome.

### 8. Safety
- Participants are encouraged to meet in the public venue confirmed at booking and to share date details with a trusted contact outside the Platform.
- Any safety concern, misconduct, or Playbook violation can be reported through the Platform's reporting mechanism; reports are reviewed under the Platform's trust & safety process.

### 9. Liability & Assumption of Risk
*(Modeled on standard marketplace-facilitator disclaimers — e.g. "we connect participants; we are not a party to what happens between them.")*
The Platform facilitates introductions, scheduling, and payment for this date; it is not a party to, and assumes no liability for, the conduct of either Participant during the date itself. Participants assume the ordinary risks of meeting a new person and agree to exercise reasonable judgment and caution.

### 10. Dispute Resolution
Disputes arising from this date instance should first be raised through the Platform's support channel for informal resolution. [Insert arbitration / governing-law clause per jurisdiction if the Platform's Terms of Service require it.]

### 11. Data & Privacy
Only information explicitly shared as part of match and booking (name, verified badges, confirmed venue/time) is disclosed between Participants. Exact contact details and precise home address are not shared as part of this plan.

### 12. Term
This Playbook governs the single date instance described in Section 2 only. It expires automatically upon completion of the date (or cancellation per Section 5) and does not carry forward to any subsequent date unless a new instance is generated.
