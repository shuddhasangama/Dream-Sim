How to delete your DhaShu account

You can delete your account at any time, from the app or the website.

In the app

  1. Open DhaShu and sign in.
  2. Tap Settings (top of Home).
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm with your password, or with a one-time code sent to your phone.

On the website

  1. Sign in.
  2. Go to /account/delete.
  3. Confirm with your password.

What happens

  • Your profile is hidden immediately and you are signed out on every device. A person you were matched with sees only "No longer available".
  • For {{ grace_days }} days you can change your mind: sign in and choose Cancel deletion.
  • After {{ grace_days }} days everything is deleted permanently: profile, stats, photos and avatars, verification images, Guru conversations, messages, files and sign-in details.
  • Payment records the law requires us to keep are kept for the legal period, with your name, phone number and email removed.
  • Security records (for example a locked account) are kept for at most {{ retention_days }} days without your identity, then deleted.
  • To recognise a returning member, we keep only a one-way scrambled (hashed) copy of your phone number, or of your email if you had no phone, and whether you left in good standing, for {{ deleted_retention_days }} days (2 years). Nobody can read your number or email from it, and it is then deleted automatically.
